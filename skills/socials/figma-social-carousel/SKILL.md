# Figma Social Carousel

Turn one useful idea into a carousel someone can read on a phone, save, and act on. Deliver researched content, coherent artwork, and a verified handoff.

This workflow comes from repeated carousel production and corrections. The latest brief and current brand assets take precedence over its defaults. Keep a campaign-specific request, such as code-only slides or a regional salary comparison, scoped to that campaign.

## Establish the brief

Inspect the supplied content, current brand files, relevant earlier decks, and available Figma write access. Capture the audience, platform, useful outcome, language, brand, slide limit, asset treatment, and CTA in a short brief. Use [assets/brief-template.md](assets/brief-template.md) when a reusable source file helps.

For a visual repair, preserve valid copy, research, and the existing sequence. Inspect and fix the affected components; research again only when a changed claim needs it. Do not restart topic selection or rewrite the deck as a side effect of alignment work.

- Limit the deck to **10 publishing slides**, counting the cover and closing slide, unless the current user asks otherwise. Publishing notes and contact sheets sit outside that count and outside the publishing sequence.
- Use **1080 × 1350**, a **64 px safe margin**, and a **32 px gap between Figma frames** as inherited production defaults. These are layout choices, not platform limits. Follow the requested format when it differs.
- Preserve an established series. Do not replace its palette, mascot, typography, or format because a design skill prefers something else.
- Resolve missing choices from the request and project evidence. Ask only when an unresolved choice would materially change the work; continue the independent research while waiting.

Read [references/content.md](references/content.md) for topic selection, research, narrative, and CTA decisions. Read [references/layout.md](references/layout.md) before designing or repairing slides. Read [references/assets.md](references/assets.md) when sourcing images, logos, or icons. Read [references/figma.md](references/figma.md) when creating or updating the Figma deliverable. For an actual RecallDeck campaign, also read [references/recalldeck.md](references/recalldeck.md).

## Select the idea before arranging slides

If the topic is open, propose a few distinct angles, identify the strongest one, and explain the choice in one sentence. Prefer a concrete problem, decision, or useful shortlist over a broad trend summary. If the user supplied a topic, develop it directly.

Choose entries for relevance, evidence, and variety of use cases. Do not fill a list with overlapping tools just to reach a round number. Verify current facts against primary sources. A popularity list needs a defined source and date; editorial picks need clear selection criteria.

Write the whole sequence before drawing. Give each slide a job, a headline, short supporting copy, and a visual that helps explain it. Make the first useful content slide stand on its own. Put a synthesis or chooser near the end when it helps the reader act.

Cut weaker entries to meet the slide budget. Combine only related items that still fit a readable comparison. A request for five items does not require five introductory or promotional slides around them.

## Compose one coherent visual system

Use a few repeatable slide types: cover, explanation, comparison or list, example, and close. Keep baselines, header position, page counters, and progress treatment consistent. Repeat the underlying spacing system while varying the composition to suit the content.

Apply the static hierarchy, typography, grouping, and restraint guidance from `apple-design` when it is installed and relevant. Use `impeccable` for a requested or useful craft review and `social` for platform strategy when available. Load their actual instructions before using them. If they are absent, follow the concrete rules in this skill; do not claim to have used them or make their installation a blocker. Static slides do not need an animation workflow.

Use one illustration or icon family for the deck. Source authentic logos, preserve their proportions, and match their apparent size. Use photography that matches the subject and series. Keep a recent-asset record so a new cover does not reuse the previous post's photograph without an intentional reason.

Show code as readable, syntax-highlighted code when the concept needs code. Use a comparison table when the reader needs to compare. Prefer a sourced or precise diagram only when it explains more than text or code. Do not fill empty space with improvised drawings.

## Make the action useful and deliverable

Choose one primary closing action: save, send, answer a specific question, or request one useful resource. A community or follow prompt can be secondary when the brief calls for it; it must not crowd the close.

For a comment-to-DM offer, prepare the exact response before writing the promise. Prefer one resource that fits in a single message. If it is a prompt, test it on a representative task and fix what fails. Do not promise an unbuilt plan, a pile of resources, instant delivery, or automation that has not been configured.

Connect a product naturally to the reader's next step. Explain what to do in ordinary language. Replace vague phrases such as "get five prompts" with an action the reader can picture, such as "write five questions about the article and answer them tomorrow."

## Build and verify the Figma handoff

Use a writable Figma integration and load any prerequisite skills required by its tools. Follow [references/figma.md](references/figma.md). Native text and layout are preferable when editable Figma content is requested. Placed PNGs are valid finished artwork, but disclose that they are flattened and keep copy editable in the source.

Inspect every slide, not just the contact sheet. Batch the first review, fix the defects together, then confirm the changed slides and full publishing order. Check:

- Text, numbers, code, pills, logos, and captions stay inside their intended bounds.
- Independent elements have visible space between them; dense rows remain separate on a phone.
- Logo size is balanced by occupied shape, not equal file dimensions.
- Light text stays readable across the actual photo crop. Contrast alone on a contact sheet is insufficient.
- Titles, counters, and progress marks match the final sequence and slide count.
- Facts, examples, syntax, source labels, dates, and numerical comparisons are correct.
- The last slide's promise matches the prepared response and the chosen platform.

Do one useful inspection and one confirmation after repairs. Extend verification only when a remaining defect justifies it; do not keep inventing polish work. A technical export check does not substitute for visual review.

## Deliver

Provide the Figma frame link when verified, ordered exports, a contact sheet, editable content source, and asset and research sources. Add concise alt text for each publishing slide and captions tuned to the requested platforms. Keep private working notes outside the publishable sequence.

Recheck current platform rules if the answer depends on character limits, image counts, audio availability, or ranking claims. Music is optional: recommend an unobtrusive passage added through the account's licensed native library, without promising its availability.

If Figma access is unavailable or fails, finish the local content and exports, supply import instructions, and state exactly what remains unsynced. If native editable Figma layers are required, that requirement remains outstanding; a PNG kit is a preview, not its completion. Do not claim local files are already in Figma. Creating artwork does not authorize publishing, sending DMs, or configuring automation.
